§01Our story

It started with a shaky phone video.

FCG — Find Connect & Grow
Founded 2025
Amsterdam, the Netherlands
Foundation (concept)

A friend working for an aid organisation sent us a video from a camp league: a fourteen-year-old dribbling past four players on a pitch made of sand and stones. Everyone who watched it asked the same question — who is scouting this kid? The answer was: nobody.

We started making calls. Clubs were interested, but nobody knew how to verify a player from a war zone, how to deal with missing documents, or how to protect a minor in such a vulnerable situation. Scouts simply don’t go there.

So we decided to build the bridge ourselves. Local scouts who know the ground. Analysts who verify everything twice. Lawyers and welfare officers who make sure every step is legal and safe. And clubs who are willing to watch.

That is FCG: find the talent, connect it to the game, and help it grow — on and off the pitch.

“Nobody chooses where they’re born. We just make sure it doesn’t decide who gets seen.”

§02Principles

Four rules we never bend.

1

The player comes first

Before the club, before the deal, before us. If a move isn’t in the player’s interest, it doesn’t happen.

2

Proof over promises

Video, data and verified documents. We never oversell a player, and never promise a family a contract.

3

Local before global

Our scouts are from the regions they work in. They are paid fairly and trained continuously.

4

We stay until the end

Not until the signature — until the player is settled. And if it doesn’t work out, we help them home or onwards.

● Safeguarding charter

Why you won’t see faces on this site.

Many of our players are minors in unsafe situations. Publishing their photos, full names or exact locations could put them at risk. So we don’t.

Generated signatures instead of photos

Every profile gets a unique contour portrait generated from its data. Recognisable, but never identifiable.

First name and initial only

Full identities are only shared with verified clubs, under NDA, after guardian consent.

Tactical replays in public

Public highlights are animated reconstructions. Real footage stays behind the club login.

Independent welfare lead

Our welfare lead reports to the board, not to the scouting team — and can stop any process at any time.

§05Nominate a talent

Know a player we should see?

Coaches, teachers, NGO workers and family members can nominate a player. A local FCG scout will follow up within four weeks.

  1. 01

    You send a nomination

    A short description and, if possible, a video link.

  2. 02

    A scout visits

    We watch the player in at least two matches.

  3. 03

    We talk to the family

    Nothing is published without their consent.
