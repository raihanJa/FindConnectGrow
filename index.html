§01The odds

Scouts go where the leagues are. Not where the talent is.

1/6children worldwide grows up in a conflict zone.

Source: Save the Children, “Stop the War on Children” (2023). More than 460 million children live in areas affected by conflict.

Every year, European clubs spend fortunes searching for the next great player. Their networks reach academies, tournaments and leagues — but they stop at the edge of the map where war begins.

Behind that edge, kids still play. On sand, on concrete, between ruins and in refugee camps. Some of them are extraordinary. Almost none of them will ever be seen.

FCG exists to close that gap. We train local scouts, verify talent with video and data, and walk every player — and their family — through a safe, legal route into European football.

Not charity. Not a shortcut. A fair chance.

§02The pathway

Four steps from a dusty pitch to a professional dressing room.

01/04
Find

Step 01 · 0–3 months

Find — local eyes, trained

We recruit and train local coaches, teachers and NGO staff as FCG scouts. They know the neighbourhoods, the camp leagues and the street tournaments where talent hides.

  • Local scout network
  • Camp & street leagues
  • Remote footage

Step 02 · 1–2 months

Verify — video, data, identity

Every player is filmed in multiple matches and assessed by two independent analysts. We verify age and identity documents and get written consent from parents or guardians.

  • 2× analyst review
  • Age verification
  • Guardian consent

Step 03 · 2–6 months

Connect — clubs meet the player

Verified profiles go live in the portal for partner clubs. Interested clubs request full dossiers and invite players for a supervised trial in Europe — visas, travel and welfare handled by FCG.

  • Club portal
  • Supervised trials
  • Visa & travel support

Step 04 · ongoing

Grow — on and off the pitch

Players who join a partner academy get education, language lessons, mental-health support and a dedicated welfare officer. We stay involved until they are settled — not just until they sign.

  • Education & language
  • Welfare officer
  • Family contact
